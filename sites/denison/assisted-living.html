Assisted Living

Assisted Living in Denison, TX

Independence, with a helping hand when it's needed — private apartments, chef-prepared meals, and caregivers on-site around the clock.

Who assisted living is for

Assisted living is a good fit for older adults who want to keep living life on their own terms but could use a helping hand with some of the day-to-day. Maybe it's keeping medications straight, getting safely in and out of the shower, or simply not wanting to cook and clean anymore. Maybe living alone has started to feel isolating — or a recent fall or hospital stay has the family worried about what could happen when no one is around.

At Live Oak Denison, assisted living means a private apartment of your own, three meals a day with friends, and a caregiver never more than a call-button away. Residents keep their independence and routines; we quietly handle the rest.

Many of the families we talk with are adult children in Denison, Sherman, Pottsboro, or farther afield who are researching care for a parent. If that's you, you're in the right place — and we're happy to talk through your situation by phone before you ever visit.

What's included

Assisted living at Live Oak Denison brings together a comfortable home, everyday support, and a full community life:

  • Private apartment with full bathroom & kitchenette
  • Medication management
  • Assistance with bathing, dressing, and grooming as needed
  • Three chef-prepared meals daily, plus snacks
  • Weekly housekeeping & linen service
  • 24/7 emergency call system in every suite
  • Care staff on-site 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Full wellness, activities, and social calendar
  • Scheduled transportation to appointments

Apartments are unfurnished, so residents can bring the furniture, photos, and keepsakes that make a place feel like home. See our floor plans for the studio, one-bedroom, and companion suite options.

A day in assisted living

There's no single schedule everyone has to follow — that's the point. A typical day might start with coffee and breakfast in the dining room, a little help getting ready if needed, and a morning activity: an exercise class, a game of dominoes, or a devotional. Lunch is a social hour. Afternoons might mean a visit from family in the living room, a book from the library, a nap in a sunny chair by the window, or an appointment with a visiting therapist. After dinner, there's music, a movie, or a quiet evening in your own apartment.

Seasonal parties, holiday celebrations, and family nights fill out the calendar, and residents are always welcome to join in as much — or as little — as they like.

Personalized care that changes with you

Every resident starts with a thorough, compassionate assessment by our care team before move-in. Together with the resident, family, and physicians, we build an individualized care plan — and we revisit it regularly as needs change. Physical, occupational, and speech therapy and physician visits are available on-site through independent providers.

Because we also offer dedicated memory care on the same campus, a resident whose needs change over time can often stay within the same community and keep familiar faces nearby.

Close to your doctors. Live Oak Denison is on Lifesearch Way, just minutes from Texoma Medical Center and the medical offices around it, with easy access to Hwy 75 for family visiting from Sherman and across Grayson County.

Safety and peace of mind

Every suite has a 24/7 emergency call system, and care staff are on-site around the clock, every day of the year. Families tell us that knowing someone is always there — at 3 a.m. as much as 3 p.m. — is what finally lets them sleep at night.

How admissions and tours work

  1. Call or send us a note. Reach us at (903) 465-5051 or through our contact form. Tell us a little about your loved one and your timeframe.
  2. Take a tour. Tours are by appointment, and after-hours tours are available for out-of-town families. Walk the building, see an apartment, and meet the team.
  3. Assessment and care plan. Our team completes an assessment and works with you and your loved one's physician to build a care plan.
  4. Pricing and move-in. We'll walk you through pricing for the floor plan and level of care that fit — clearly, with no surprises — and help plan a comfortable move.

Have questions about cost, insurance, or how assisted living differs from a nursing home? Our frequently asked questions page covers the basics.
