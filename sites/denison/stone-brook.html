A New Chapter

Stone Brook Assisted Living is now Live Oak Denison

Same address on Lifesearch Way. New name, new ownership, and new leadership.

What's changed

The assisted living and memory care community at 1616 Lifesearch Way in Denison, Texas — formerly known as Stone Brook Assisted Living — is now under new ownership as Live Oak Denison (Live Oak Denison LLC), a Live Oak Senior Care community.

With the new name comes new leadership and a renewed commitment to the families of Denison, Sherman, Pottsboro, and all of Grayson County: care that's rooted in compassion and grounded in the way we'd want our own parents to be cared for.

What's the same

  • The same location: 1616 Lifesearch Way, Denison, TX 75020
  • Assisted living and dedicated memory care on one campus
  • Minutes from Texoma Medical Center and Hwy 75

Come see us

Whether you knew the community as Stone Brook or you're discovering it for the first time, we'd love to show you around. Tours are available by appointment, including after-hours visits for out-of-town families.

Call us at (903) 465-5051, request a tour online, or follow along on Facebook.
