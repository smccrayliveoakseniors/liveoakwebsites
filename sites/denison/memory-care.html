Memory Care

Memory Care in Denison, TX

A secure, purpose-built community for people living with Alzheimer's and dementia — with certified staff, gentle routines, and care around the clock.

Who memory care is for

Memory care is designed for people living with Alzheimer's disease or another form of dementia whose needs have grown beyond what assisted living — or home — can safely provide. Families often start looking when a loved one begins wandering or getting lost, becomes confused about time and place, struggles with daily routines like eating and bathing, or when the strain on a spouse or adult child caregiver has simply become too much.

If you're noticing these changes, you're not alone, and you don't have to figure it out by yourself. Our team is glad to talk through what you're seeing and help you decide whether memory care is the right next step.

Alzheimer's-certified memory care in Denison

Memory care at Live Oak Denison is a secure, purpose-built community within our campus, with staff certified in Alzheimer's and dementia care. Everything — from the daily rhythm to the design of the space — is meant to reduce confusion and anxiety and to help each resident feel safe, capable, and at home.

What's included

  • Private suite with full accessible bathroom
  • Secure environment with access to a walkable courtyard
  • Dementia-informed design & lighting for easier navigation
  • Specialized memory care staff, 24 hours a day
  • Help with bathing, dressing, and grooming as needed
  • Medication management
  • Three chef-prepared meals daily, plus snacks
  • Structured daily activities designed for mind and memory

A day in memory care

People living with dementia do best with gentle, predictable routines. Days in our memory care community follow a familiar rhythm — meals at consistent times, a mix of small-group and one-on-one activities, and plenty of time for rest. Music, singalongs, simple crafts, sensory activities, and time outdoors in the secure courtyard help residents stay engaged and calm.

Our caregivers get to know each resident's life story — their career, family, faith, favorite songs, and daily habits — and use it to connect, redirect, and comfort. Small moments of joy are the goal of every day.

A safe, secure environment

Safety is the foundation of good memory care. Our memory care community is secured to help prevent wandering while still giving residents freedom to move about, including access to an enclosed, walkable courtyard. Dementia-informed design and lighting make it easier to find the way, and care staff are present around the clock.

Close to medical care. We're on Lifesearch Way in Denison, minutes from Texoma Medical Center, which makes appointments and specialist visits easier for residents and families from Sherman, Pottsboro, and across Grayson County.

Support for families

Dementia affects the whole family. We see families as partners: we'll keep you informed, include you in care planning, and welcome your visits. When needs change, we'll talk with you openly about what we're seeing and how we're adjusting care.

Because Live Oak Denison also offers assisted living, couples with different care needs can often live on the same campus, and residents whose memory needs increase can often transition within a community they already know.

How admissions and tours work

  1. Start with a conversation. Call (903) 465-5051 or send us a message. We'll listen to what's happening and answer your questions honestly.
  2. Visit. Tours are by appointment, with after-hours options for out-of-town families. You're welcome to bring your loved one, or to visit on your own first.
  3. Assessment. Our team completes an assessment and coordinates with your loved one's physician to make sure memory care is the right fit and to build an individualized care plan.
  4. A gentle transition. We'll help you plan the move — including what familiar items to bring — so the first days feel as comfortable as possible.
