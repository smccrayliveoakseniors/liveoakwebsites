Welcome Home

Quality care, delivered the way we'd want it for our own families.

Live Oak Denison is an assisted living and memory care community at 1616 Lifesearch Way in Denison, Texas — formerly Stone Brook Assisted Living, now under new ownership with a new name and new leadership. We're here for the families of Grayson County: a place where residents are known by name, days are filled with purpose, and care is always close at hand.

We're an easy drive for family from Sherman, Pottsboro, and across Texoma, just off Hwy 75 and minutes from Texoma Medical Center.

  • Assisted living & dedicated memory care
  • Care staff on-site 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Chef-prepared meals in a warm dining room
  • Meaningful daily activities & wellness programs

Close to Home
