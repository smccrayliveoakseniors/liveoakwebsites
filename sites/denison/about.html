About Live Oak Denison

A community built for our community.

We've spent years learning what makes senior living feel like home. Live Oak Denison is where we're putting it all into practice.

Our Story

Rooted in compassion. Grounded in care.

Live Oak Senior Care was founded on a simple belief: that the later chapters of life deserve as much thought, beauty, and intention as any before. Our founders spent decades watching their own families navigate senior living — the good, the disappointing, and the moments they wished had been different.

So we set out to build something better. A place where caregivers know every resident's story. Where the food is made by people who love to cook. Where families feel welcomed, not inconvenienced.

Live Oak Denison is the next chapter of that mission — and our first community in Grayson County.

What We Believe

Our values, in practice.

These aren't just words on a wall. They guide every hire, every meal, every decision we make.

1

People first, always

Every resident is known by name, and every family is a partner in their loved one's care.

2

Dignity in every detail

Private rooms. Private bathrooms. Real food. Beautiful spaces. Little moments matter.

3

Local, not corporate

We live here, too. Our staff is our neighbors, and your family is ours.

4

Care that adapts

Needs change. We meet them — with care that grows alongside your loved one.

5

Purposeful days

Meaningful activity, social connection, and a reason to get up each morning.

6

Transparent partnership

Clear pricing, clear communication, and open doors. No surprises.

Why Denison

Close to home, for the families who belong here.

Grayson County families shouldn't have to choose between great senior care and staying close to home. For too long, that's been the trade-off. Live Oak Denison is here to change that.

We chose Lifesearch Way because of what surrounds it — an established, growing community where generations of families have put down roots. A place worth staying.

  • Minutes from Texoma Medical Center and area physicians
  • Easy access for family from Sherman, Pottsboro, and across the Red River into Texoma
  • Close to the shops, restaurants, and services along the Highway 75 corridor
Formerly Stone Brook

A new name on a familiar building.

The community at 1616 Lifesearch Way, formerly Stone Brook Assisted Living, is now Live Oak Denison — under new ownership, with new leadership and the same commitment to Denison families.
